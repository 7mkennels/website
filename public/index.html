About us

We're a big family living out in the country in Northeast Georgia, and our Labs are a part of everyday life here. Our goal is to raise sweet-tempered, healthy Labrador Retrievers that thrive just as well in the field as they do at home with their families.

Our puppies come from high-quality bloodlines, and every one of them receives daily attention and care from the day they're born until the day they go home with you.

Raised with our family

We moved to rural Northeast Georgia 13 years ago and fell in love with the area and its people. As our family grew, we wanted a project we could all be involved in, and raising Labs has been an exciting adventure that all of our children love.

Every child helps care for the puppies, so each one grows up well acclimated to people of all ages and to plenty of everyday adventures. Our hope is to see every puppy find a loving family and a place to call home.

We are committed to raising healthy, happy, intelligent and well-adjusted puppies.
