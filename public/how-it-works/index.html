How buying a puppy works

The same steps for every family, in the order they happen.

  1. Inquire

    Send the inquiry form and tell us about your home and what you want in a dog.

  2. Talk with Katy

    Katy will answer any questions you have about the puppies, the parents or the process.

  3. Choose and reserve

    Pick from the available puppies. A $500 deposit locks in your reservation and goes toward the total price. Before go-home day, Katy sets up video calls so you can get to know your puppy's personality.

  4. Meet and take them home

    Pick up at the Atlanta airport or in Lavonia, or we ship your puppy at your cost. Registration is provided at sale.
